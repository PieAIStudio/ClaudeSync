# Art sources

The four `group-*.png` marks were generated for ClaudeSync on 2026-10-25 with the image tool in Codex CLI (gpt-6.1-sol), from our own earlier ClaudeSync art as the style reference. No third-party images or stock assets were used.

The 3D icons under `icons-3d/` are byte-for-byte copies of the approved HQ export `tool-ui-claude-20261025/icons-3d/export/claudesync/`. They are bundled locally at 128, 256 and 512 pixels; the export manifest and the delivery receipt record their SHA-256 values.
